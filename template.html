A tag counts as answered when the tagged person adds any comment on the same lead, or replies in the comment thread, after the tag. Calls and texts alone don't count. Repeat tags of the same person on the same lead are grouped into one row. Automation tags are comments posted by workflows, such as "Review this lead" or "Lead Moved to Follow-Up". Clearing a lead only hides it here; nothing changes in REsimpli. A cleared lead comes back if someone is tagged on it again. Review soon marks waiting tags on leads in an active stage (New Leads, Make Offer, Potential Deal, Under Contract). No reply needed marks waiting tags on leads that are dead or rejected.
